The Process

How Roof MRI Works

From training to final report, we've built a system that makes moisture detection straightforward for your team.

1

Get Certified

Your team completes Roof MRI's training program and earns their certification. We teach the methodology, the equipment handling, and the scanning protocols that produce reliable results every time.

2

Scan the Roof

Using Roof MRI's patent-pending process, your trained crew performs a moisture scan on-site. The scan is non-destructive and integrates into your existing workflow with minimal disruption.

3

We Build the Report

Upload your scan data and our team takes it from there. We analyze the results and deliver a professional, client-ready moisture report you can hand directly to building owners and property managers.

4

Win the Bid

Walk into your next bid meeting with concrete moisture data and a certified report that sets your proposal apart. Property managers trust data, and Roof MRI gives you exactly that.
